Your Inbox

Inbox shows recent videos from your subscriptions as YouTube loads them. The default window is 14 days; choose 1–30 days in settings. Older videos remain in Subscriptions. Videos you explicitly move to Inbox stay there regardless of age.

Save or dismiss the videos you have seen. Once you reach the end of your window, Inbox shows “All caught up.”

Save, Dismiss and Undo

Save to Watch later adds a video to your signed-in YouTube account. The card leaves Inbox while Watchbox confirms the save. If confirmation fails, check Watch later before retrying. Saving currently supports English desktop YouTube menus.

Dismiss hides a video from Inbox on this browser. Choose Undo in the action message to bring it back, or use Move to inbox in Subscriptions later. Remove account saves through YouTube’s Watch later playlist.

On a video page

Save position remembers where you stopped. Watched marks the video watched and dismisses it. Focus hides the video sidebar while you watch. Progress and watched status are saved manually.

Choose what appears

Shorts and related videos are hidden by default. Change individual filters in settings. Hide search suggestions removes extra suggestion shelves while keeping matching video results. Redirect to Inbox works when the Home feed is hidden and Subscriptions is visible.

Turn Watchbox enabled off to restore YouTube without erasing your decisions, saved positions or settings.

Your data

Video details, Inbox decisions, saved positions and settings stay in this browser. Watch later saves belong to your YouTube account.

Need help?

When reporting a problem, include your Watchbox version, browser version, YouTube language and the steps to reproduce it. The Watchbox version appears at the bottom of settings.
